TM 9053 touchINTERACTIVE — look and feel options

CIDP biomarkers activity · prepared by Forged Frameworks for the Touch team · draft, 17 Nov 2026

Storyboard v1 notes that the visual design is confirmed at the next stage. To make that step quick, this page sets out four looks the activity could take, drawn from what the client and the publisher already use, and three icon styles. Each option shows the same sample screen: stage 1 of the CIDP journey, with the storyboard's own wording.

  1. A · Sanofi corporate — from sanofi.com.
  2. B · The VML brief — from the two-pager the client supplied (built on Sanofi's own PowerPoint theme).
  3. C · Middle ground — B's purples and challenge/application colours on a light, readable base.
  4. D · touchNEUROLOGY, with the brief's wave — the publisher's look, as used for the Sanofi-sponsored CIDP activity already on touchneurology.com, with the two-pager's journey wave redrawn as the background.

To look closer: select an option in the bar below to see it on its own. On its sample screen, the journey stages and the "Reveal next" button work.

One idea runs through every option: the two-pager already codes challenges with a warm peach puzzle badge and potential biomarker applications with a cool lilac lightbulb. Keeping that coding on every journey stage tells the learner where they are at a glance, whichever look is chosen.

Contrast figures under each option are measured in this page against WCAG 2.2 (4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text and controls). Fonts marked "stand-in" are shown in a similar font because the brand files are not held.

On every sample screen, stage 1 shows the card text: select an underlined abbreviation for its meaning, or a citation number for its reference (S8 in the notes). Wording, abbreviations and references are from storyboard v1 (11 Nov), screen 10.

Iconography

Storyboard v1 mixes three icon styles (below, left). Choosing one family keeps the screens calm and lets the challenge/application coding do the work.

Style 1 · Line icon in a round badge Recommended

The two-pager's own style, carried across every stage. Works in A, C and D by changing two colours. Light, crisp at small sizes, clear on phones.

Style 2 · Duotone in a rounded tile

Bolder; matches Font Awesome duotone, which touchNEUROLOGY already loads. Best with option D.

Style 3 · Scientific illustration, for science moments only

The storyboard's biomarker timeline pictures. Suggested for the timeline scene only, beside style 1 elsewhere. Their source and licence need confirming before use.

What Forged Frameworks suggests

  1. Look: the touchNEUROLOGY look (the publisher's, as on the Sanofi-sponsored CIDP activity), carrying the brief's peach/lilac coding for challenges and applications. With the brief's journey wave as the background, for impact and recognition: option D. If the client prefers visible Sanofi alignment, option C.
  2. Icons: style 1 throughout; style 3 only on the biomarker timeline.
  3. Not advised: B as built (moving gradient, glass panels and large background videos), for readability over 15–20 minutes and for phone performance. D keeps its motif without those costs.

Needed to confirm a look: whether client branding is permitted on the activity; the brand pack if A or C is chosen; the Sanofi Sans web-font files; permission to reuse two-pager imagery.
