Entrance behaviour - two candidates, 22 September 2026

A test copy only. The live activity is unchanged at tint8787-omnipod-scenes, scenes v11.

What is being tested. In the Visme player a scene's entrance animations have already played by the time you arrive at the slide. Two different fixes are published here. Put each on its own slide in a blank Visme project, then move back and forth and see which one animates on arrival.

A - held until shown, replays on return

  • 03-demographics - seven cards and counting numbers
  • 04-challenges - five cards you open yourself

Waits to be told it is actually on screen before animating, and animates again each time you come back. Measured off the real player this trigger never fires, so this one may show no animation at all - that result is just as useful as a pass.

B - no hold, animations paused while parked

  • 03-demographics
  • 04-challenges

What the Lilly CLL activity does, and that one animates correctly on arrival. The scene draws straight away and its entrance is held at the first frame while the slide is parked.

What to look for, on each of the four

  • Arrive at the slide - does the content ease in, or is it already settled?
  • Move away and come back - does it ease in again?
  • On 04-challenges, open two or three cards, leave, come back - are they still open? They should be. Losing them is a failure.
  • On 03-demographics, the numbers must not count up again from zero on a return.

Each page carries a coloured label in its top-left corner naming the variant and the scene, so a slide pointing at the wrong copy is obvious.
