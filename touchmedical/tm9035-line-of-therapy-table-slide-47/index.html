VISION trial — tepotinib in METex14 skipping NSCLC

Data are for the 313 patients with METex14 skipping NSCLC in the Phase 2 VISION trial. ORR is objective response rate; mDOR is median duration of response. 95% CIs are shown for tepotinib efficacy outcomes; IQR is shown for prior treatments. Viteri S, Felip E, Smit E, Veillon R, Le X, Raskin J, et al. 1995P Treatment (Tx) sequencing of tepotinib in patients (pts) with MET exon 14 (METex14) skipping non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC): Outcomes from ≥3 years follow-up of the VISION study. Ann Oncol. 2025;36:S1060–S1061. 18
