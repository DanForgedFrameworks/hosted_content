Efficacy outcomes

ORR, DOR and PFS in Asian patients

?

Compared with the overall population of the VISION study, what effect did line of treatment and METex14 skipping detection methods have on ORR, DOR, PFS and OS in Asian patients?

Outcomes were consistent across METex14 skipping detection methods and lines of treatment

Outcomes in Asian patients with METex14 skipping NSCLC were consistent with the overall population of the VISION study, with clinically meaningful efficacy observed across treatment lines and METex14 skipping detection methods.

?

Did patient baseline characteristics impact ORR in Asian patients with METex14 skipping NSCLC?

No – ORR was consistent across all patient baseline characteristics

Consistent efficacy was observed irrespective of baseline characteristics shown below including in patients aged ≥75 years and those with brain metastases at baseline.
