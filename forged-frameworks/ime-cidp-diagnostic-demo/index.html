Stage 1 of 4 · Clinical pattern

Read the presentation and mark the criteria it satisfies

A 58-year-old woman presents with four months of steadily worsening difficulty climbing stairs and opening jars. Examination shows symmetric weakness of hip flexion and shoulder abduction (MRC 4-) and of finger extension and ankle dorsiflexion (MRC 4). Vibration and pinprick sensation are reduced in both hands and both feet. Tendon reflexes are absent throughout. No pain, no bulbar or respiratory symptoms.

    Stage 2 of 4 · Motor nerve conduction

    Mark each parameter that reaches the demyelinating threshold

    Limits are shown under each value. Tick a cell where the measurement crosses the threshold in that nerve. The engine then counts nerves per parameter.

    Thresholds (illustrative): distal motor latency ≥50% above the upper limit; conduction velocity ≥30% below the lower limit; F-wave latency ≥20% above the upper limit; conduction block ≥30% proximal-to-distal amplitude drop, tibial nerve excluded.
