Key points

Every patient with advanced nonsquamous lung cancer should have EGFR testing at diagnosis, as part of broad biomarker testing.

Testing works best as a team

The pulmonologist or radiologist aims to take enough tissue for diagnosis and molecular testing. The pathologist saves tissue and, where policy allows, sends it automatically (reflex testing). The oncologist checks results are back before starting treatment.
