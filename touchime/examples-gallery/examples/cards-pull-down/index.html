  • The pulmonologist or radiologist aims to take enough tissue for diagnosis and molecular testing.
  • The pathologist saves tissue and, where policy allows, sends it automatically (reflex testing).
  • The oncologist checks results are back before starting treatment.
  • A blood test looks for tumor DNA in the bloodstream (ctDNA).
  • It is useful when tissue is limited or a biopsy is not possible, or alongside tissue to speed up results.
  • A positive result can guide treatment.
  • A broad panel is preferred.
  • It tests EGFR and other targetable genes at the same time, which saves tissue and time.
  • RNA sequencing is the preferred way to find gene fusions.
