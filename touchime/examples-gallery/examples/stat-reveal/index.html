Biopsies are often small, and a repeat biopsy adds risk and delay.

A broad panel is preferred. It tests EGFR and other targetable genes at the same time, which saves tissue and time.

A blood test looks for tumor DNA in the bloodstream (ctDNA). It is useful when tissue is limited or a biopsy is not possible, or alongside tissue to speed up results.
