Three short videos, on coercive control, bullying at work and sexual harassment, and a guide to sexual harassment. Press play to watch the videos here. Subtitles are available in the player.

  • Hidden in plain sight: coercive control

    Hidden in Plain Sight was created using genuine experiences and testimonies of survivors and focuses on coercive control tactics and behaviours. By telling personal stories, the animation aims to help viewers understand that coercive control is a pattern of behaviour that happens over time, has long-term consequences and distorts reality for the victims. It is meant to be used as a training tool, to explore:

    • controlling behaviour and tactics
    • the impact and consequences on women, children and young people
    • challenges in seeking help
    • challenges in identifying the perpetrator's behaviour

  • Sexual harassment

    A short film from HASSL, a UK campaign against harassment, about the everyday harassment many women and girls face, and the rules they feel they have to follow to stay safe.

    Sexual harassment is unwanted behaviour of a sexual nature. It can be comments, jokes, touching, images or messages, at TIRO, at work or online. One incident can be enough, and "it was only banter" is not an excuse.
