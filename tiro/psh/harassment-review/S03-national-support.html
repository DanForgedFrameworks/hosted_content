Urgent In an emergency

If you or anyone you know is in immediate danger, a threat to life or at risk from criminal activities, do not hesitate to ring 999.

Call 999

Then contact a Designated Safeguarding Lead as soon as it is safe to do so.

These are some of the national support services and organisations that can help you find the assistance you need. For services near you, use Regional support.

Go to regional support

Links to other websites open a short check first, so you can see where you are going.
