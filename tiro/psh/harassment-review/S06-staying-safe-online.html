10 steps to staying safe online

Work through these checks on each of your devices. They are updated for 2026, including scams that use AI. They build on Elie Bursztein's guide and current advice from the National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC).

  1. Keep your devices, apps and security software up to date. Turn on automatic updates.
  2. Back up your important files and photos.
  3. Use passkeys wherever a site or app offers them.
  4. Use a password manager for everything else, with a strong, different password for each account.
  5. Turn on two-step verification for important accounts. An authenticator app is safer than a text message.
  6. Keep your account recovery details, such as your phone number and email, up to date.
  7. Take browser and app security warnings seriously. Do not click past them.
  8. Stop and check any unexpected request for money, codes or personal details, even from a voice or face you know. AI can clone voices and fake video, so confirm another way.
  9. Think before sharing personal details, photos or work documents with AI chatbots and apps, and check your privacy settings.
  10. If anyone shares, or threatens to share, intimate images of you, real or made with AI, do not pay or reply. Tell a Designated Safeguarding Lead.
